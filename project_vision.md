# Crafted Co Vision

## What is Crafted Co?

Crafted Co is the digital home of everything I build.

It documents the journey from idea to finished product.

It is a place where projects evolve publicly through development logs, screenshots, experiments, failures and releases.

Visitors are not only seeing finished work.

They are seeing the process behind it.

---

# Why Crafted Co Exists

Most portfolios only show the final result.

Crafted Co shows both

The destination

and

The journey.

The goal is to create a living archive that grows for years.

---

# Audience

People interested in my work.

Recruiters.

Developers.

Designers.

Friends.

Future me.

Every visitor should immediately understand

"I build things."

---

# What Crafted Co Is NOT

Not an agency.

Not a SaaS landing page.

Not a startup website.

Not a marketing funnel.

Not an online resume.

Not a social media replacement.

---

# Core Identity

Builder.

Maker.

Designer.

Developer.

Problem Solver.

Learner.

Everything on the website should reinforce these ideas.

---

# Long Term Vision

Over the years Crafted Co should naturally grow into a library of

Mobile Apps

PC Games

Websites

Experiments

---

# Design Goals

The website should feel

Premium

Minimal

Creative

Personal

Authentic

Calm

Intentional

Never corporate.

Never loud.

Never trendy for the sake of trends.

Timeless over fashionable.

---

# Content Philosophy

The work speaks first.

The design supports the work.

The interface should never overpower the content.

Every page should have a clear purpose.

Every section should justify its existence.

---

# Writing Style

Simple.

Honest.

Technical when needed.

Avoid buzzwords.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Avoid marketing language.

Write naturally.

The journal should read like development notes, not advertisements.

---

# Project Philosophy

Every project deserves its own identity.

Each project page should tell a story.

Problem

↓

Idea

↓

Development

↓

Challenges

↓

Current State

↓

Future Plans

Rather than simply listing features.

---

# Journal Philosophy

The journal is the heart of Crafted Co.

Every meaningful development session should be documented.

Entries may include

New features

Bug fixes

Design iterations

Technical discoveries

Pixel art progress

Game development

Failures

Lessons learned

Small progress matters.

Consistency is more important than long posts.

---

# AI Development Guidelines

Whenever making design decisions

Prioritize clarity.

When making architecture decisions

Prioritize scalability.

When making UI decisions

Prioritize usability.

When making animation decisions

Prioritize subtlety.

When making performance decisions

Prioritize speed.

When uncertain

Choose the simpler solution.

# Success Criteria

A successful Crafted Co website should make visitors think

"This person genuinely enjoys building things."

rather than

"This person is trying to impress me."

The website should age well.

Every new project should feel like adding another chapter to an ongoing story rather than another item to a portfolio.
